# 🎤 KrishiSense Pitch Script (Speaker Notes)

This document contains the speaking script for the KrishiSense presentation. It highlights what is visible on each slide alongside the exact words you should say as a speaker.

---

## Slide 1: Title Slide
* **On Slide:** "KrishiSense: Open Soil Health Monitoring Network" / "Real-time soil intelligence. ₹500 per node. Open for all."
* **Script:** 
> "Good [morning/afternoon]. We are Team [Name]. 14 crore Indian farming families depend on their soil for survival, but they have almost zero real-time information about its health. Today, we are going to change that."

---

## Slide 2: The Problem - The Fertilizer Death Spiral
* **On Slide:** India spends ₹1.9 TRILLION annually on fertilizer subsidies. Punjab's NPK ratio is 31:8:1 (Ideal is 4:2:1).
* **Script:** 
> "Imagine going to the doctor once every 5 years. That's how often our soil is tested. Because farmers lack real-time data, they blindly apply subsidized urea. In Punjab, farmers apply 8 times more nitrogen than needed. It's a death spiral: more fertilizer degrades the soil, lowering yields, causing them to apply even more fertilizer."

---

## Slide 3: Why Existing Solutions Fail
* **On Slide:** Govt Soil Health Cards (Free but take months). Commercial IoT (Real-time but costs ₹15,000–30,000/year).
* **Script:** 
> "Existing solutions don't work for the 86% of farmers who own less than one hectare. Government cards are free but take months to arrive. Commercial IoT platforms cost upwards of ₹15,000 a year. There is a massive gap for a real-time, ultra-affordable solution."

---

## Slide 4: Introducing KrishiSense
* **On Slide:** The ₹500 Soil Scientist. 1. Ultra-Cheap Hardware 2. Live Dashboard 3. Open Ecosystem. 
* **Script:** 
> "Enter KrishiSense. It’s a ₹500 sensor node—a realistic, affordable cost including the microcontroller—which is still cheaper than a single bag of premium fertilizer. It connects to a live dashboard that translates complex data into simple colors: Green means good, Red means act now. And most importantly, it is completely open-source."

---

## Slide 5: How It Works (The Science)
* **On Slide:** Flowchart showing Arduino/Sensors → Weighted Health Score (Moisture 50%, Temp 25%, Humidity 25%) → Alerts.
* **Script:** 
> "The science is simple but effective. Our algorithm weighs soil moisture at 50% because it is the most actionable metric for a farmer. The baselines are mapped to standard agronomic sweet-spots. If moisture drops below 30%, the system instantly flashes a red alert both on the device LEDs and the dashboard."

---

## Slide 6: The Hardware & Live Demo
* **On Slide:** Built for the Field. Details on capacitive sensors (no exposed metal, lasts 6-18 months).
* **Script:** 
> "We specifically chose capacitive sensors because they don't corrode in wet Indian soil. Let's see it live. [Perform Demo]. As I pour water into this dry soil, you can see the red LED instantly turn green, and the dashboard score jumps to 85. Instant feedback."

---

## Slide 7: Business & Revenue Model
* **On Slide:** 3 Tiers (Free open hardware, Freemium API, Enterprise B2B deals for crop insurers/fertilizer cos).
* **Script:** 
> "How do we make money if the hardware is open? The data is the product. Open access builds the largest real-time soil dataset in India. We monetize through B2B deals—crop insurers pay for field data to process claims faster, and fertilizer companies pay for precision regional insights."

---

## Slide 8: Triple Bottom Line Research & Impact
* **On Slide:** Economic (ICAR/FAO - 15-20% yield increase), Environmental (IWMI - 20-40% water savings), Social (ICRISAT - smallholder empowerment).
* **Script:** 
> "We didn't just guess these numbers; they are grounded in research. ICAR and FAO studies confirm that precision agriculture boosts yields by up to 20% while saving farmers thousands on fertilizer. IWMI data shows we can cut water use by 40%. And socially, following ICRISAT's models, we know that putting open data directly into the hands of smallholders empowers entire communities."

---

## Slide 9: Competitive Moat
* **On Slide:** Self-reinforcing open-source loop. Proprietary hardware vs. our community-deployed hardware and data platform.
* **Script:** 
> "Our competitive advantage is our open-source loop. Proprietary companies have to sell expensive hardware. We let the community deploy the hardware, and we build the data platform. More nodes mean better data, which brings in more partners."

---

## Slide 10: Roadmap & The Ask
* **On Slide:** Phase 1 (USB) → Phase 2 (Wi-Fi) → Phase 3 (LoRa). Asking for FPO introductions and cloud credits.
* **Script:** 
> "Today, you see our working Phase 1 prototype. In 6 months, we move to Wi-Fi pilots, and eventually to LoRa mesh networks that require no cellular towers. Today, we are asking for your support to connect us with Farmer Producer Organizations to run our first 10-node field pilot."
